# 10x Genomics

> 10x Genomics is a life sciences technology company building instruments, reagents, and software that resolve biology at single-cell and spatial resolution (Chromium single-cell, Visium spatial transcriptomics, Xenium in situ). It ships command-line analysis pipelines and open-source tooling rather than a public REST developer API.

## Products & Platforms
- [Chromium Single Cell Platform](https://www.10xgenomics.com/platforms/chromium): Single-cell gene expression, immune profiling, and multiomics.
- [Visium Spatial Platform](https://www.10xgenomics.com/platforms/visium): Whole-transcriptome spatial gene expression.
- [Xenium In Situ Platform](https://www.10xgenomics.com/platforms/xenium): High-plex single-cell resolution in situ spatial biology.

## Developer & Software Surface
- [Cell Ranger](https://www.10xgenomics.com/support/software/cell-ranger/latest): Analysis pipelines for Chromium single-cell data.
- [Software Support & Documentation](https://www.10xgenomics.com/support): Downloads, docs, and analysis guides for all software.
- [GitHub Organization](https://github.com/10XGenomics): 76+ open-source repositories (cellranger, spaceranger, loupeR, scan-rs) — informational source, not officially supported clients.
- [Datasets](https://www.10xgenomics.com/datasets): Public single-cell and spatial datasets.
